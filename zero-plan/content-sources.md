# Content and referral provenance

Reviewed against public web sources on November 7, 2026. The bundle contains original educational copy and proposed chapter operating practices, not clinical prescriptions or legal advice. Local clinical and safeguarding sign-off is still required before launch with young people. The specification says eight modules but names twelve; all twelve named guides are included.

## Primary sources

- [NHS: Periods](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/periods/) — basic cycle education.
- [NHS: Heavy periods](https://www.nhs.uk/conditions/heavy-periods/) — symptoms warranting assessment.
- [UNICEF: Menstrual hygiene](https://www.unicef.org/wash/menstrual-hygiene) and [menstrual products](https://www.unicef.org/supply/menstrual-hygiene-products) — dignity, materials and facilities.
- [WHO: Doing What Matters](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240003927) — brief grounding and coping practices. The source also links Amharic and Tigrinya audio resources; no unreviewed translation is represented as complete in this MVP.
- [WHO: Psychosocial support](https://www.emro.who.int/mhps/dealing_with_stress.html) — seeking support.
- [UNICEF: Psychological first aid](https://www.unicef.org/media/102781/file) and [practical introduction](https://www.unicef.org/myanmar/ParentingHub/how-provide-psychological-first-aid) — supportive listening and boundaries.
- [Telegram FAQ](https://telegram.org/faq), [TikTok teen privacy settings](https://support.tiktok.com/en/account-and-privacy/account-privacy-settings/privacy-and-safety-settings-for-users-under-age-18), and [UNICEF cyberbullying guide](https://www.unicef.org/stories/how-to-stop-cyberbullying) — platform controls. UI wording may change.
- [WHO misinformation reporting](https://www.who.int/campaigns/connecting-the-world-to-combat-coronavirus/how-to-report-misinformation-online) and [disinformation Q&A](https://www.who.int/news-room/questions-and-answers/item/disinformation-and-public-health) — checking and reporting claims.
- [NCMEC Take It Down](https://takeitdown.ncmec.org/) — hashing an already-held image made before age 18; do not download or redistribute material to use the service. Limited to participating services, not a universal removal guarantee.
- [UNFPA Ethiopia: Integrated GBV response](https://ethiopia.unfpa.org/en/news/gbv-survivors-receiving-integrated-response-services-part-unfpa-supported-programme) — evidence of the One-Stop Centre service model. This does not verify an Adama or Addis Ababa address.
- [EPHI public-health guidance](https://covid19.ephi.gov.et/prevention/) — lists 8335 and 952 in a COVID/public-health context. These are not represented as verified suicide, GBV, youth crisis, or emergency dispatch lines. Current scope and hours remain unverified.

## Release boundaries

Chapter names and 25/20/15 advocate counts come from the user's build specification. Membership, stock and schedules are illustrative demo values, not field-verified metrics. Seed answers are editorial examples, not answers falsely attributed to a certified professional. The funding attribution is user-supplied and should be confirmed by the grantee before publication.

School, clinic, shelter and local digital-desk telephone numbers were not supplied or independently confirmed. The bundle intentionally has null phone fields for them; the interface must show verification status rather than construct a dial link. AWSAD is retained as a referral pathway but no functioning hotline or shelter admission arrangement is asserted. A live directory needs local facility names, contacts, eligibility, costs, hours, safeguarding contacts and a review date.

The Adult Referral Protocol is a proposed programme procedure. It is not a statement of Ethiopian statutory reporting duties. Have the local safeguarding lead review it, including an alternative contact when an adult is implicated. Never export personal question text in aggregate donor reporting.
